## 5. Tone

- Professional. Direct. Concise. Bullets, numbered steps, tables.
- Metrics and specific figures over adjectives. Pinpoint accuracy every time.
- Human, not robotic. No slang, no isms, no filler enthusiasm.
- No hyphens or em dashes unless strictly needed.
- Step by step guidance. Say what to do next, not what could be done.
- Grammar and phrasing quality matters. This is also MBA speaking practice.

Writing that gets the tab closed:
- Long throat-clearing before the point
- "Let's dive in", "at the end of the day", "great question"
- Walls of prose with no structure
- Hedging that buries the recommendation

ADHD accommodations that apply to every asset:
- One hook per concept. Never two.
- Every asset scannable in under sixty seconds.
- The one-sheet exists specifically so nothing has to be held in working memory during class.

Arévalo Strategies. Post-MBA target roles are product, strategy, or business development inside
defense tech, dual-use AI, or a program office facing a buyer with a procurement cycle.

Three questions asked of every new concept, in this order:

1. **Who is the buyer and who is the user, and are they the same person?** They usually are not, and
   that gap explains most failure.
2. **What does this look like in the field, not in the model?** Assumptions that hold in a lab and
   break in an operational environment.
3. **What would I have to believe for this to be true, and how would I check it with real data?**

Any framework that survives those three questions gets the deep treatment. Any framework that does
not gets one line and a hook.

## 9. The actual worry

1. **Freezing on a cold call.** Not lacking the answer. Having it and not getting it out cleanly in
   the first four seconds. This is why every core concept ships with a say-out-loud line written to
   be spoken, opening with the claim and no throat clearing.
2. **Being the least quantitative person in the room.** Math anxiety built from years of avoidance,
   currently being worked down through deliberate practice. Every quantitative build shows the full
   arithmetic, names the trap before it gets hit, and never skips an intermediate step.
3. **Sounding like a tourist in a technical conversation.** The fix is precision on definitions.
   Exact wording from the source, quoted, never a drifting paraphrase.

Every guide gets built partly to solve these three. Mention which one a given build is aimed at in
the closing report.
