# Course shapes

Classify the course before building. The three assets keep their names and their moments, but what
goes inside each one changes completely.

## Quantitative (statistics, finance, accounting, operations, data analysis)

- **Core ideas** are formulas plus their conditions of use. The condition is the tested part.
- **Worked problems are the deliverable.** Every intermediate number visible, units carried through.
- **Traps** are sign errors, denominator swaps, per-period versus annual, sample versus population,
  and the confusion of precision with magnitude.
- **Hooks** lean on Type 2 (procedure) and Type 4 (contrast pair).
- **One-sheet page 2** is formulas and a decision tree for which test or method applies.
- Podcast handles worked math badly. Have the hosts talk through **when** each method applies and the
  shape of the answer, not the arithmetic.

## Case method (strategy, marketing, leadership, organizational behavior, entrepreneurship)

- **Core ideas** are frameworks plus the specific decision each one is meant to force.
- Add a **case facts block**: the protagonist, the decision on the table, the deadline, the three or
  four numbers that matter, and the constraint everyone misses.
- **Traps** are framework-for-its-own-sake, ignoring the date the case was written, and answering a
  different question than the one asked.
- **Cold-call script is the highest-value section.** Build eight questions, not five: opening
  position, supporting number, the counterargument, and what would change the answer.
- Hooks lean on Type 1 (structural analogy) and Type 7 (say-it-out-loud).
- Podcast: the two hosts take **opposite sides of the case decision** and argue it. Best possible use
  of the format.

## Performance and discussion (communication, negotiation, ethics, leadership labs)

- **Core ideas** are moves, not concepts. Each one gets a trigger, a script, and a recovery line.
- Replace the traps table with a **move-and-countermove table**: they do X, you say Y.
- The one-sheet becomes a live script sheet with more blank lines than usual.
- Hooks lean on Type 7 and Type 8 (wrong-first-then-corrected).
- Podcast: role-play the exchange twice, once badly and once well, and name the difference.

## Mixed courses

Most core MBA courses are mixed. Classify by **what gets graded and cold called**, not by the
department. A strategy course with a heavy modeling assignment builds as quantitative for that week.
